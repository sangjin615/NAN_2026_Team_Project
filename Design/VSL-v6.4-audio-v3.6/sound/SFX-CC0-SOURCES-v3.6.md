# SFX CC0 source record · Audio v3.6

Audio v3.6 mixes edited real Foley at roughly 70–80% with a short procedural support body at roughly 20–30%. The runtime WAV files are derivative game-ready edits; the original archives are not required by the game.

All source families below were marked CC0 on their OpenGameArt pages when retrieved on 2026-08-03.

- **Ticking clock** — bart  
  https://opengameart.org/content/ticking-clock

Material allocation:

- UI and bid controls: small wood contacts, restrained mechanical clicks
- Auction and relic auction: wood hammer, paddle, cloth, metal seal
- Office and settlement: book/page Foley, small metal appraisal tools
- Exchange: coins, beads, wood tokens, abacus-like contacts
- Guild: locks, keys, metal springs and mechanisms
- Merchant house: tools, ratchets and compact wood mechanisms
- Deadline/loading ambience: real clock ticking under a low procedural room bed

Attribution is not required by CC0, but retaining this record is recommended for production provenance.
